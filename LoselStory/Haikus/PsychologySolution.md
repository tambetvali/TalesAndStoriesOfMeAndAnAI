# Family of Vice
## A Multidimensional Critical Essay
### (Psychological • Thermodynamic • Game-Theoretic • Taoist • Narrative • Philosophical)

---

# 1. Essence of the Narrative

This story is not primarily about family.

It is about a disagreement concerning the nature of psychology itself.

Losel and Victor unconsciously assume that human decisions emerge from:

- likes,  
- dislikes,  
- preferences,  
- emotions,  
- comfort.  

Vice argues something radically different.

According to Vice, certain psychological trajectories exist independently of desire.

Just as a fruit ripens whether it wants to or not, a human life possesses developmental stages.

Among these stages are:

- separation,  
- individuation,  
- exploration,  
- reconciliation,  
- recollection,  
- return.  

Meeting a dying father therefore becomes, in Vice's eyes, neither an emotional preference nor a moral duty.

It is a natural completion of narrative structure.

The encounter is simply where the river was already flowing.

---

# 2. Psychology Beyond Likes and Dislikes

Most people explain human behavior through immediate preferences:

> I like this.  

> I dislike that.  

> I feel good.  

> I feel bad.  

Vice rejects this framework.

He suggests that psychology operates on deeper layers.

A person may dislike visiting their family.

A person may be anxious about returning.

A person may even resent their father.

Yet the reunion can still be psychologically necessary.

Psychology here resembles biology.

A child may dislike growing teeth.

An adolescent may dislike maturation.

An old person may dislike aging.

The process continues nevertheless.

Vice therefore views reconciliation not as a preference but as maturation.

---

# 3. Narrative Psychology

Vice's argument closely resembles narrative psychology.

Humans do not merely live.

Humans construct stories.

Life events gain meaning when integrated into a coherent narrative.

The absent father becomes a loose thread.

The distant homeland becomes an unfinished chapter.

The childhood friends become unresolved memories.

The psychological pressure comes not from society.

It comes from narrative incompleteness.

The psyche seeks closure.

Not because closure is pleasant.

But because unfinished structures generate tension.

The journey home therefore resembles the final chapters of a novel.

---

# 4. Thermodynamic Interpretation

The entire story becomes particularly interesting when viewed through thermodynamics.

## 4.1 Psychological Potential Energy

Unresolved relationships behave like stored energy.

For years there may exist:

- unanswered questions,  
- unspoken apologies,  
- unrealized understandings,  
- unfinished narratives.  

These accumulate psychological tension.

In thermodynamic language:

    Unresolved past
    =
    Stored potential energy

The longer the distance persists, the greater the potential difference.

A reunion partially discharges that energy.

The system moves toward equilibrium.

---

## 4.2 Why Celebration Happens

Vice says:

> When I go there, it will be a celebration.

Victor asks whether he actually likes celebrations.

Vice's answer is deeper than it first appears.

Celebration is not the cause.

Celebration is the consequence.

When separated systems reconnect, energy is released.

Examples:

- Water finds level.  
- Electricity equalizes voltage.  
- Families reunite.  
- Friends reconnect.  

The celebration is the social manifestation of equilibration.

People laugh.

Stories are exchanged.

Memories are relived.

Energy that remained frozen for years begins flowing again.

---

## 4.3 Why Celebrations End

Vice makes an apparently humorous observation:

> One day is celebration.  
> Two days is a bigger celebration.  
> A week or month begins war or betrayal.

This is thermodynamically profound.

Temporary reunions generate positive energy because they are rare.

Permanent proximity generates friction.

Every system has costs.

Every human interaction consumes energy.

Every household produces entropy.

Therefore:

    Distance
    → longing

    Extreme distance
    → alienation

    Moderate reunion
    → celebration

    Permanent reunion
    → friction

Vice understands that celebration itself depends upon limitation.

---

# 5. Repeated Games Analysis

The story can also be interpreted through repeated-game theory.

## 5.1 Family as the Longest Game

Most social relationships are temporary.

Family relationships are unusual.

The game extends over decades.

Sometimes over entire lifetimes.

The players remember previous rounds.

The score never completely disappears.

A childhood argument may survive forty years.

A small kindness may survive equally long.

In repeated games, cooperation becomes valuable because interactions continue.

Family therefore contains informational depth unavailable elsewhere.

---

## 5.2 Victor's Error

Victor argues:

> They do not know you.  
> We know you.

Vice implicitly disagrees.

Victor knows the current Vice.

The adult Vice.

The working Vice.

The present-state Vice.

But family and childhood friends may possess access to earlier states.

They know:

- childhood fears,  
- original ambitions,  
- family patterns,  
- developmental history.  

In game-theoretic language:

Victor observes recent rounds.

Family observed the opening rounds.

Neither perspective is complete.

Both contain information.

---

## 5.3 Why Vice Trusts Old Connections

Repeated games are not measured only by frequency.

They are measured by depth.

A colleague may see a thousand days of work.

A childhood friend may see twenty years of formation.

An employee knows your role.

A father knows your origins.

A friend knows your ambitions before success, failure, money, status, ideology, and compromise.

Vice therefore sees childhood relationships as carrying information unavailable to later observers.

The game began long before the current players entered.

---

# 6. Wu Wei Analysis

The deepest layer may be Taoist.

## 6.1 The River Returns to the Sea

Losel analyzes.

Victor worries.

They attempt to reason:

- Should Vice go?  
- Does he like it?  
- Is it useful?  
- Is it rational?  

Vice largely ignores these questions.

This is classical Wu Wei.

He is not forcing the event.

He is not resisting the event.

He simply recognizes its arrival.

The father is dying.

The relationship remains unfinished.

The path appears.

Therefore he walks it.

Nothing more is required.

---

## 6.2 Non-Resistance

Wu Wei is often misunderstood as passivity.

Actually it means alignment.

Vice's behavior resembles a leaf floating downstream.

The leaf does not choose the river.

The leaf does not fight the river.

The leaf follows the existing flow.

Returning to family becomes the path of least resistance.

Not because it is pleasant.

Not because it is profitable.

Because reality itself points there.

---

## 6.3 Vice's Psychological Flow

The psychological flow may be summarized:

    Childhood
    → Separation
    → Exploration
    → Work
    → Distance
    → Reflection
    → Reconciliation
    → Closure

Vice is simply executing the next stage.

The flow itself carries him.

---

## 6.4 Why Vice Does Not Explain Himself

An important feature of Wu Wei is that it rarely seeks justification.

Losel seeks understanding.

Victor seeks reassurance.

Vice offers explanations, yet seems unconcerned whether they convince anyone.

He behaves as if the event has already happened psychologically.

The river has already chosen its direction.

The discussion itself becomes secondary.

This is perhaps the strongest Wu Wei element in the story.

Vice is not trying to optimize opinions.

He is trying to follow reality.

---

# 7. Philosophical Interpretation

## 7.1 Against Consumer Psychology

The modern world often presents identity as preference:

> I am what I like.

Vice rejects this.

For him:

> I am the story I am completing.

This is a fundamentally different philosophy.

---

## 7.2 Identity Through Origins

One cannot fully understand a tree by examining only the branches.

Roots matter.

Family represents roots.

Childhood represents roots.

Origins represent roots.

Vice seeks not nostalgia but understanding.

---

## 7.3 The Tragedy of Victor

Victor's concern is not malicious.

It emerges from affection.

Yet he reduces human connection to present utility.

His logic becomes:

    We help you now.
    Therefore we know you.

Vice's logic becomes:

    They were there before I knew myself.
    Therefore they know part of me nobody else can.

Both perspectives contain truth.

The conflict emerges because each sees only one side.

---

# 8. Reality Behind the Dialogue

The story may also be interpreted as a conflict between three realities.

## Losel's Reality

Reality is understanding.

Things become real when motivations become visible.

He asks why.

He investigates causes.

He attempts to construct explanatory models.

---

## Victor's Reality

Reality is current relationships.

The people who help you today are the people that matter.

Present interactions dominate historical ones.

The visible network becomes reality.

---

## Vice's Reality

Reality is developmental.

People move through stages.

Parents age.

Friends separate.

Journeys expand.

People return.

The story is larger than any individual preference.

Vice does not ask what he likes.

He asks where in the story he currently stands.

# 10. Closing Reflection

This story is not really about whether Vice likes his father.

Nor about whether he likes celebrations.

Nor even about whether family relationships are good or bad.

It is about the difference between preference and process.

Vice treats psychology as a developmental phenomenon rather than a collection of emotions.

From a thermodynamic perspective, old tensions seek equilibrium.

From a repeated-game perspective, family contains the deepest and longest sequence of interactions.

From a narrative perspective, unfinished chapters seek completion.

From a philosophical perspective, origins remain part of identity.

From a Taoist perspective, returning is simply what rivers do.

The Wu Wei of Vice is therefore remarkably simple.

He does not return because he wants to.

He does not return because he is obligated.

He does not return because others approve.

He returns because the river has reached the point where flowing home is the natural continuation of its course.

The psychology, in Vice's view, was never a matter of likes and dislikes.

It was the shape of the story itself.
